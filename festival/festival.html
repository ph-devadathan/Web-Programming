Festivals in India

India's festivals are diverse, including religious celebrations like Diwali (Festival of Lights), Holi (Festival of Colors), Eid, and Durga Puja, as well as major national events such as Independence Day and Republic Day. Other prominent festivals are Ganesh Chaturthi, Onam, Pongal, and the religious observance of Krishna Janmashtami.
